🚨 Spec-driven development gets very interesting when you compare quality per dollar.

I compared two generated BigConfig polyglot package specifications:

🔵 gpt-5.5 xhigh: $4.599
🟢 ds4 flash xhigh: $0.024
⚡ DS4 Flash was about 192× cheaper, saving $4.575 on this run.

🧾 Fair assessment in one sentence:
The DS4 Flash version is more concrete and implementation-oriented around BigConfig’s current lifecycle, options, and parameter-resolution model, while the GPT-5.5 version is cleaner as a product/ecosystem specification with stronger terminology, CLI, release-gate, roadmap, and risk framing.

💡 My takeaway:
The expensive model gave more polish and strategic structure.

🔁 The cheap model gave enough useful depth to iterate fast.

📉 For spec-driven development, cost per iteration matters as much as raw model capability.

That is the shift I care about:

🤖 Agents do not need one perfect answer.
✅ They need a cheap, repeatable loop that converges under validation and review.
